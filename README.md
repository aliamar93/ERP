# 🎓 Student Overtime Management System

A web-based system to manage **student overtime shifts** through a structured approval and registration process. It ensures secure email-based signup, streamlined shift creation, and transparent student participation tracking.

## 🚀 Module 1: Student Signup & Shift Management

### ✨ Features

#### 🔐 Student Signup
- Students enter their email on the signup page.
- **Email is validated via JavaScript.**
- A unique **signup URL** with a **personal number** is sent to the student's email.
- Students use the link to:
  - Complete profile setup
  - Register in the system

#### 🕒 Shift Creation (Shift In-Charge)
- Shift in-charge creates a shift by providing:
  - Date
  - Time (From – To)
  - Departments involved
- Shift is sent for approval to the **Department Head**.

#### ✅ Approval & Email Notifications
- Upon department head's approval:
  - System sends an **overtime registration link** to eligible students.
  - Link requires:
    - Personal number
    - Available time slot for the shift

#### 📝 Student Overtime Registration
- Students click the email link and submit their availability.
- Data is stored securely in the system database.

#### 👨‍💼 Shift Participation Overview
- Shift in-charge can view the list of **interested students** before the shift day.
